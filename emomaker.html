How to make a piece

Six simple steps.

The kit is built for Procreate, but the template also opens in Photoshop. Draw each trait on its own layer over the fixed body base.

1

Download the kit

Grab the template, both palettes, and the Emonad Brush from the section below.

2

Open the template

3000 × 3000, every trait layer named and ready. Don't resize the canvas.

3

Pick a trait slot

Hair/Hats, Mouth, Eyes, Clothes, Tattoos, or Background. The body + nose are fixed, so draw on top, don't redraw them.

4

Draw with the Emonad Brush

Linework at 12% (the default). Size down for fine details if you want, then flat-fill with palette colors only.

5

Export a transparent PNG

Hide every layer except yours. Name it like hair-spikes.png. Submitting a full 1/1? Include all your trait layers so the whole character exports together.

6

Submit

Send it through the form below. Tag your X handle for credit.

FAQ

Common questions.

What tool should I use? +

Procreate. The kit (template, palettes, and the Emonad Brush) is built for it. The .psd template also opens in Photoshop if that's your thing, and the Emonad Brush is the only brush to use.

Can I submit multiple pieces? +

Yes. As many as you want. Each as its own PNG, named clearly. Quality over quantity though.

What's a trait vs a 1/1? +

A trait is one element (eyes, mouth, hair, accessory) that gets combined with a base + other traits to generate PFPs. A 1/1 is the whole character drawn together. It ships as a single unique mint. For a 1/1, still use the trait slots, and add a custom background if you want.

Do I get credit if my piece is selected? +

Yes. Selected submitters get credit on the collection page and GTD WL. Exact details get announced before mint.

I'm not a great artist. Help? +

Start on the template to get a feel for the canvas, then try a simple trait: sunglasses, a tear, etc. Use the Emonad Brush and stay on palette and it'll already look like it belongs.

When does the collection mint? +

Wen it's done.
