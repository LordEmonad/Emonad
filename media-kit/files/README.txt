EMONAD MEDIA KIT
emonad.lol/media-kit

Everything for working with Emonad, at full size.

FOLDERS
logo/        the EMO mark. emo-mark.svg is the master (vector, any size).
             -white, -black, -purple are one colour with the letters cut out: for print, embroidery, stamps,
             and anything that sits on a photo or a colour.
character/   Emonad from the front, three-quarter, side and back, and his head. SVG (vector) and 4000 px PNG.
cat/         the cat, with and without his crown. SVG and 4000 px PNG.
colour/      the palette: .ase (Adobe, Affinity), .json, .txt and a sheet to look at.
banners/     X header (1500x500 @2x), link card (1200x630 @2x), profile pictures (1000x1000).
ABOUT.txt    what to say about $EMO, the contract and the links.

THE LOGO
- Use the files. Never retype EMO in a font, redraw it, stretch it, outline it or add effects to it.
- Full colour on Night, white or any light background. On a photo, Emo Purple or anything busy: the white one.
- Leave clear space around it of at least a quarter of its width.
- Smallest: 24 px tall on screens, 12 mm in print. Below that the brush strokes close up.

EMONAD
- Purple hair over his right eye, always the same eye. White skin, black outline.
- Black tee, black skinny jeans, purple wristbands with white stripes, purple sneakers with white toes.
- Deadpan by default. Big feelings about small things.
- Change his pose, his expression, his surroundings. Keep his colours and his hair.

TYPE
Anton for headlines (capitals, set tight). Space Grotesk for everything else. Both free on Google Fonts.

COLOUR
Night #08070B, Emo Purple #522B5F and White carry the brand. Lilac #B894D8, Violet #9B5FFF (screens only),
Pink #E84D7F and Ash #8A8496 are seasoning.
